## What was measured

`benchmarks/lifecycle_benchmark.cpp` (fields, corrected semantics):

- `compile_ms` — ModelIR→CompiledModel compilation only. **Excludes** StateDB
  allocation, TetPlan construction and initialization (those happen in the
  untimed `CpuRun` construction / `initialize()` on the next lines).
- `kernel_ms` — the **first full `advance()`** (load application, force
  evaluation+assembly, integration, cursor/time update). Not kernel-only.
- `steady_kernel_ms` / `steady_kernel_per_increment_ms` — 8 further full
  advances (increments 2–9), away from first-touch effects.
- `nondue_snapshot_ms` — `snapshot()` at a non-due increment (increment 1).
- `snapshot_ms` — `snapshot()` at a due pure-U increment (increment 10).
- IO: `native_write_ms` (1 frame), `reader_scan_query_ms`,
  `restart_write_ms`, `restart_restore_ms`.
- introspection: `force_evaluations`, `peak_process_bytes` (budget-enforced),
  `execution_policy:"serial"`, `omp_compiled`, `omp_max_threads`, `omp_env`.

Generated model: 21-increment plan, output frequency 10; the benchmark
executes **10 increments** (1 + 8 steady + 1) and writes **1 frame**; each
JSON row records `plan`/`executed` counts only implicitly via this file
(fielded counters are added in the repair commit for future runs).

## R1.01 measured effect (same conditions, serial, A/B)

- Non-due publication: **1.39 ms → 0.014 ms (200k)**, **8.59 ms → 0.015 ms
  (1M env1)** — the removed cost equals ~44–50% of one full serial advance
  (the redundant force pass), now ~free.
- Due pure-U snapshot: −1.5/−1.6 ms (200k), −8.1/−8.2 ms (1M).
- Steady advancing, cold compile, and all IO timings unchanged within noise.
- `force_evaluations` 14 → 12 in-benchmark, matching R1.01 unit-contract
  counts.

## Honest observations recorded for later batches (corrected)

- ~~OMP4 ≈ serial (bandwidth-bound)~~ **RETRACTED (B01-A-001/O-002)**: the
  lifecycle benchmark cannot execute OpenMP at all (CpuRun has no policy
  parameter). **No parallel-efficiency or bandwidth conclusion exists for the
  lifecycle path.** A real OpenMP lifecycle baseline requires a deliberate
  public API change (R2 candidate); the low-level `sdb_benchmark` does select
  `CpuPolicy::OpenMP` explicitly.
- `reader_scan_query_ms` ≈ `native_write_ms`: the reader still scans all
  frames (known R4 target) — this observation is from serial runs and stands.
- Whole-run solver acceleration is **not** claimed from these segmented
  measurements; process-wall differences (~0.6% at 200k) are within protocol
  noise on this desktop.
